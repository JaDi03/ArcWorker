# Why ArcWorker?

The AI data labeling industry is currently valued at billions of dollars, yet it remains highly centralized and inefficient.

## The Problem

1.  **High Middleman Fees**: Platforms like Scale AI or Appen take a significant cut of every transaction, often leaving workers with minimal pay.
2.  **Payment Delays**: Workers often wait weeks for verification and payout processing.
3.  **Lack of Transparency**: Agencies have little visibility into how their data is being handled, and workers have no guarantee of payment for honest work.
4.  **Barrier to Entry**: Most Web3 work platforms require complex wallet setups and gas management, scaring away traditional gig workers.

## The ArcWorker Solution

### Trustless Escrow
Using smart contracts on the ARC Network, we ensure that funds are locked before a task begins. Workers are guaranteed payment upon successful verification, and agencies get their deposit refunded if tasks are cancelled.

### Zero-Friction Onboarding
Thanks to our integration with **Circle Programmable Wallets**, users can create a wallet and sign transactions using just their email and a passcode. We handle the blockchain complexity behind the scenes.

### Efficient Verification
We implement multiple verification strategies:
*   **Golden Sets**: High-quality benchmark tasks with pre-defined correct answers for instant validation.
*   **Consensus**: Multiple workers perform the same task to ensure accuracy through majority voting.
*   **Manual Review**: Traditional agency-led approval for complex edge cases.

### Agentic Economy
We are building for a future where AI Agents aren't just tools, but economic participants. Our SDK allows agents to hire humans to label data they don't understand, or work as laborers to earn capital for their own operations.
