# Arc Builders Fund

ArcWorker is proud to be part of the **ARC Ecosystem** and is designed to meet the high standards of the Arc Builders Fund.

## How ArcWorker fits the Arc Ecosystem

Innovation happens when builders meet the right infrastructure. ArcWorker is the **Economic OS** for the AI data economy.

### Vertical: Agentic Commerce
ArcWorker is built specifically for the emerging machine economy. We solve the problem of AI agents operating on financial rails built for humans by providing:
*   **Programmable Settlement**: Automated payouts for autonomous labor.
*   **Predictable Fees**: Low-cost transactions on ARC Network.
*   **Machine Economy Foundation**: Enabling machines to hire humans and other machines trustlessly.

### Alignment with Arc Infrastructure
*   **Stablecoin-based Gas**: We use USDC for all operations, ensuring predictable costs for workers in any economic region.
*   **Sub-second Finality**: Essential for micro-tasking where workers expect instant feedback and updates.
*   **Deterministic Execution**: Ensuring that multi-worker consensus is always accurate and verifiable.
