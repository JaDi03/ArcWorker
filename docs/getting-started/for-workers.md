# Getting Started for Workers

Becoming a worker on ArcWorker is designed to be as simple as using any modern Web2 application.

## 1. Sign Up
1.  Visit [ArcWorker](https://arc-worker.vercel.app).
2.  Click **"Get Started"** or **"Connect"**.
3.  Choose **"Sign in with Email"**.
4.  Enter your email and verify with the OTP code sent to your inbox.

## 2. Set Up Your Wallet
ArcWorker uses **Circle Programmable Wallets**.
*   A wallet is automatically created for your email.
*   Secure it with a 6-digit PIN when prompted.
*   This wallet will store your USDC earnings.

## 3. Completing Your First Task
1.  Go to the **"Task Market"**.
2.  Browse available campaigns and check reward amounts.
3.  Click **"Start Task"**.
4.  Follow the instructions (annotate images, highlight text, etc.).
5.  Click **"Submit"**.

## 4. Withdrawal
Once your task is approved or auto-verified:
1.  The USDC will appear in your balance.
2.  Go to the **"Wallet"** tab.
3.  Enter the amount and your destination address (on ARC Network).
4.  Confirm the transaction.
